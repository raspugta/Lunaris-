01 — The problem

Where the sun
never rises.

Near the lunar poles, crater floors have not seen direct sunlight for billions of years. These permanently shadowed regions are among the coldest places in the solar system and may trap water ice. They are also dark, steep and poorly mapped, which makes them very hard to drive into.

02 — The approach

Inspect. Understand.
Prioritise. Navigate.

LUNARIS fuses laser altimetry, thermal models, illumination maps and orbital imagery into layers you can trace back to their source. Every layer is labelled measured, derived, model estimate or illustrative, and unknown terrain is never treated as safe.

03 — Capabilities

Six instruments. One console.

A

Provenance

Checksums, CRS, units and missing-metadata flags for every NASA product ingested.

B

Terrain

Slope, roughness and hazard classes from 20 m LOLA elevation, checked against NASA's own slope map (r = 0.998).

C

Ice priority

Transparent evidence scoring from PSR, Diviner temperature and ice stability. Candidates, not claims.

D

Navigation

Admissible 8-neighbour A* over a documented cost surface, with rejected alternatives explained.

E

VIPER and Perseverance-class rovers driven with Bekker–Wong terramechanics: slip, sinkage, tip-over, energy.

F

Mission brief

PDF and JSON exports with sources, assumptions, uncertainty and limitations.
